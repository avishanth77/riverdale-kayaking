Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about kayaking in Kadalundi Mangrove Forest with Riverdale Kayaking.

Is kayaking in Kadalundi safe for beginners and non-swimmers?

Yes, 100%! The mangrove backwaters of Kadalundi and Balathiruthi are tranquil and protected from strong ocean currents. We provide certified high-buoyancy life jackets (PFDs) for adults and kids, ultra-stable sit-on-top kayaks that do not flip easily, and certified local guides accompany every tour. Non-swimmers paddle safely with us every single day!

What is the best time for mangrove and sunrise kayaking in Kadalundi?

Our Sunrise 6:00 AM slot is the most magical. The water is as smooth as glass, morning mist hangs over the mangrove root canopy, temperatures are cool, and you will spot dozens of migratory bird species active in the Kadalundi Bird Sanctuary. Our 4:30 PM Sunset slot is also magnificent for golden-hour photography.

Where exactly is the launch dock located?

Our launch dock is located at Balathiruthi, Kadalundi, Kozhikode, Kerala 673634. It is just 3 km from Kadalundi Railway Station and 18 km from Calicut International Airport (CCJ). Free parking is available for two-wheelers and cars right at our launch dock.

What gear is provided, and what should I wear?

We provide single and tandem sit-on-top kayaks, lightweight paddles, certified life jackets, and waterproof dry bags for your mobile phones and cameras. Please wear comfortable, quick-dry clothes or sportswear (avoid heavy denim). Slippers or water sandals are ideal. Bring sunglasses, sunscreen, and a change of clothes if you wish.

How do I reserve a slot for my group or family?

Click the "Book Your River Trip" button anywhere on the page, select your preferred tour, date, and number of kayakers, and submit your reservation. We will confirm your slot immediately with a booking reference ID. You can also contact us directly at +91 98470 12345.
